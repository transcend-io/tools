Add `transcend consent deploy`, `transcend consent preview-deploy`, and `transcend consent rollback`. `consent deploy` deploys the Consent Manager bundle without changing the airgap.js version. The SDK gains `rollbackConsentManager`, `fetchConsentManagerDeployState`, and `waitForConsentManagerDeploy`.
